03 / A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Clarity first.
Then a first step.

Is QualiFi live today?+

QualiFi is in pilot development. You can request a pilot or join the waiting list here. Live AI analysis and automatic agent activation are not available yet.

Will my email or WhatsApp connect immediately?+

At this stage we collect your channel preference only. We do not access your account; integration scope is defined during your pilot discussion.

Do I have to upload a contract?+

No. You can request a pilot with a sample agreement. If you add your own, PDF or TXT files up to 5 MB are accepted and saved only when you submit your request.

Are cash forecasts guaranteed?+

No. Forecasts depend on the available data and assumptions. Example screens are not live analysis or guarantees of collection.

How we use your information

Your email, optional company/role, solution and channel preferences are stored on the application server to process your request. Your own agreement is saved only when you submit the request.

At this stage your document is not sent to an AI model, and your email/WhatsApp accounts are not connected. The waiting list is for product/pilot updates; pilot and meeting requests do not automatically subscribe you.

With your permission, page and button interactions are measured with a random visitor ID. Emails and document contents are not included in analytics. Forms work if you decline measurement; submissions may include your chosen solution and campaign source.
